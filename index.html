How this search works

Ask in plain words — a word, a topic, or a full question. The search understands topics, not just exact words: “lights” also finds lighting, illumination and exterior fixtures; asking about washing your car also looks at vehicles, driveways and water. Every result shows its source — document, section/rule, and page — with the exact rule text, so you can check it yourself.

Results start with an answer: an AI (running free on Cloudflare) reads the top passages the search found and writes a plain-English answer from only those passages, with citations — if the passages don't answer your question, it must say so instead of guessing. Under it is a hand-written short answer for the main topics, then the passages themselves. Your question and the passages are sent to the AI to write the answer; nothing is stored.

The search itself runs entirely on your phone or computer, and neither it nor the AI decides what is allowed — they find and summarize the passages, and the documents themselves are the final word. When in doubt, ask the association / FirstService Residential.

Install it like an app

On your phone, open this page in your browser and choose “Add to Home Screen” (Share menu on iPhone, ⋮ menu on Android). It installs as a web app — no app store, no account.
