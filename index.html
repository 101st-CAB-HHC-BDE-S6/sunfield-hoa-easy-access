How this search works

Ask in plain words — a word, a topic, or a full question. The search understands topics, not just exact words: “lights” also finds lighting, illumination and exterior fixtures; asking about washing your car also looks at vehicles, driveways and water. Every result shows its source — document, section/rule, and page — with the exact rule text, so you can check it yourself.

Results start with a short answer: for the main topics, a plain-English summary written by hand from the very passages shown below it, with citations — if the documents don't directly address your question, it says so instead of guessing. For other questions, it pulls the key requirement sentences from the top matches. There is no AI behind it; the summaries never go beyond what the cited passages say.

This is a smart keyword/topic search that runs entirely on your phone or computer. It is not an AI and it does not decide what is allowed — it finds the passages, and the documents themselves are the final word. When in doubt, ask the association / FirstService Residential.

Install it like an app

On your phone, open this page in your browser and choose “Add to Home Screen” (Share menu on iPhone, ⋮ menu on Android). It installs as a web app — no app store, no account.
