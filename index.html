How this search works

Ask in plain words — a word, a topic, or a full question. There are no pre-built answers and no keyword shortcuts: an AI (running free on Cloudflare) reads your question, thinks about what it really concerns, and searches the rule books itself — it has a map of every part of every document and picks the parts that govern your question, even when the documents use different words than you did. It then writes a plain-English answer from only the passages it found, with citations — if those passages don't answer your question, it must say so instead of guessing.

Under the answer are the passages it used, each with its source — document, section/rule, and page — in the exact rule text, so you can check it yourself. Your question is sent to the AI to do the searching; nothing is stored. Neither the site nor the AI decides what is allowed — the documents themselves are the final word. When in doubt, ask the association / FirstService Residential.

Install it like an app

On your phone, open this page in your browser and choose “Add to Home Screen” (Share menu on iPhone, ⋮ menu on Android). It installs as a web app — no app store, no account.
